The chain the authority will read, computed in front of you

Five determinations, each one derived from the one before it, each one traceable to the clause that produced it.

STEP 01 iGRC Characteristic dimension and the population under the flight geography. Class 1–10.
STEP 02 · M1 / M2 GRC Ground mitigations applied at their evidenced robustness. Final ground risk class.
STEP 03 ARC Airspace class, altitude band, aerodrome proximity and traffic. a, b, c or d.
STEP 04 Residual ARC Strategic mitigation by operational restriction or common structure.
STEP 05 · GRC × ARC SAIL I–VI Sets the ~24 OSOs at Low, Medium or High robustness, plus containment.
01 — TRACE

Every line cites its rule

The derivation tree holds the whole determination. Each node names the rule that fired and the clause it comes from; the raw facts you confirmed sit at the leaves. A case officer can follow it without asking you a question.

02 — GEOGRAPHY

Draw the flight geography. The volumes follow.

Contingency volume and ground risk buffer are derived from your aircraft's speed, altitude and response times, not typed in. Population density and ED-269 geo-zones sit underneath, so the adjacent area is visible before it becomes a finding.

03 — DOSSIER

The output looks like it came from an aviation professional

ConOps, the SORA assessment tables, the OSO matrix with evidence references, and an ERP skeleton. Versioned, rulebase-stamped, exported as DOCX or PDF.

Disclaimer

SORA Copilot prepares and verifies your risk assessment. Your national aviation authority grants the authorisation.

The determinations this service produces are decision support. They are computed from the facts you confirm, against a stated version of the rulebase, and they are shown with the reasoning that produced them so that you can check it. They are not an authorisation, a declaration, or a legal opinion, and they do not transfer any part of the operator's responsibility under Regulation (EU) 2019/947.

You remain the accountable party for the accuracy of the facts you enter, for the evidence you attach to each OSO, and for the operation itself.
